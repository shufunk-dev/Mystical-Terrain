# Mythic Armor Sets & Set Bonuses

Defeating the Realm Colossi provides the ingredients needed to craft complete mythic armor sets. Equipping matching armor sets unlocks unique set bonuses that enhance damage, grant elemental immunities, or summon guardian familiars.

---

## 🛡️ Notable Armor Sets

### Celestial Aegis Armor Set
*The apex starlight armor forged from Astrael's celestial drops.*
- **Full Set Bonus**: **Cosmic Barrier** – Automatically generates a protective starlight shield that absorbs up to 40% of incoming damage and deflects explosive projectiles.

*Spun from the shadowy threads of the Hollow Warden.*
- **Full Set Bonus**: **Veil of Shadows** – Grants complete invisibility and movement speed while crouching in light levels under 7, and nullifies Darkness/Blindness debuffs.

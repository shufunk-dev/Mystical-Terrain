# Unique Weapon Archetypes & Progression

Mystical Terrain equips players with mythic weapon archetypes beyond standard swords and axes. Every colossus yields materials used to forge distinct weapons featuring custom active skills, charge bars, and combo finishers.

---

## ⚔️ Colossus Signature Weapons

### Nova Greatsword
*Forged from Astrael's celestial essence and netherite.*
- **Skill**: **Supernova Slash** – Right-click to discharge a wide wave of starlight that detonates on impact and pierces through enemy shields.
- **Weapon Class**: Greatsword (Heavy, High Reach, Knockback Resistance)

### Soulreaper Scythe
*Infused with the souls of fallen phantoms.*
- **Skill**: **Soul Harvest** – Slain foes drop soul orbs that empower your next basic attack, stacking up to 5 times.
- **Weapon Class**: Scythe (Wide Sweeping Arc, Life Steal)

### Glacial Lance
*Chiseled from the frozen heart of Hrimthane.*
- **Skill**: **Frost Piercer** – Thrust forward in a linear dash that freezes hit enemies in solid ice blocks for 3 seconds.
- **Weapon Class**: Lance (Charge Attack, Frost Infliction)

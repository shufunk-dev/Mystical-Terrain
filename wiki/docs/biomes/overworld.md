# Overworld Sanctuaries

The Overworld in Mystical Terrain features breathtaking natural wonders, dense ancient forests, towering mountain ranges, and vibrant floral meadows.

## 🏔️ Titan Peaks
- **Climate**: Cold / Alpine Mountain
- **Flora**: Rimewood trees, glacial moss, alpine edelweiss
- **Terrain**: Jagged snow-crested limestone spires ascending past $Y = 240$. Shrouded in continuous snowfall and howling blizzards.
- **Fauna**: Frost ravens, mountain goats, and roaming frost elementals.
- **Notable Colossus**: **Hrimthane, The Frozen Warlord**.

## 🌸 Blossom Vale
- **Climate**: Temperate / Lush
- **Flora**: Mystwood cherry blossoms, duskbells, starflowers, and glowing petals
- **Terrain**: Rolling pink and lavender meadows crisscrossed by clear spring-fed streams and illuminated water lilies.
- **Special Effect**: Ambient floral drift particles that restore minor health when lingering under elder blossom canopies.
- **Notable Colossus**: **Lirael, The Blooming Seraph**.

## 🌲 Ancient Redwoods
- **Climate**: Humid Temperate
- **Flora**: Colossal procedural Redwood & Grand Cypress trees
- **Terrain**: Deep humus soil, fallen hollow nurse logs, and thick fern understories. Canopies reach heights of 60 to 90 blocks.
- **Resources**: High-yield timber, amber deposits, and primal beast dens.

## 💎 Crystal Highlands
- **Climate**: Temperate / Celestial
- **Flora**: Silverbark trees, moonpetals, and crystal geodes
- **Terrain**: Polished granite plateaus studded with protruding amethyst and moonstone formations.
- **Special Mechanics**: Amplified lunar visibility and increased starlight absorption.

## 🌾 Amber Meadows & Maple Woods
- **Climate**: Warm Autumnal
- **Flora**: Ginkgo, Maple, marigolds, goldenrod, and pumpkin patches
- **Atmosphere**: Golden sun shafts, falling crimson leaves, and rich harvest foraging.

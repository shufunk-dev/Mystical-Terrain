# Nether & The End Realms

Venture beyond the mortal Overworld into scorched hellscapes and starry void reaches.

## 🔥 Nether: Ember Wastes & Scorched Badlands
- **Ambient Fog**: Deep ember orange and smoke particulate
- **Flora**: Charwood trees, fire lilies, magma blooms
- **Terrain**: Black ash plains, basalt pillars, and wide lava deltas.
- **Hazards**: Scorching heat zones and erupting cinder vents.
- **Ruler**: **Pyraxis, The Infernal Sovereign**.

## 🍄 Subterranean & Nether: Fungal Depths
- **Ambient Fog**: Bioluminescent cyan and toxic spore motes
- **Flora**: Sporewood giants, giant puffballs, luminous mushrooms
- **Terrain**: Damp mycelium shelves and spore falls that cascade into cavernous pits.
- **Atmosphere**: Disorienting spore clouds that require protective respirators or hollow shrouds.
- **Ruler**: **Mycelord, The Fungal Spore Patriarch**.

## 🌌 The End: Astral Spires & Starlit Voids
- **Skybox**: Prismatic cosmic nebulae and drifting stardust
- **Flora**: Starbark trees, celestial voidblooms, crystalline chorus variants
- **Terrain**: Inverted floating islands connected by gravity filaments and star bridges.
- **Colossus**: **Astrael, The Starlit Emissary**.

# Landscape Formations & Anomalies

Beyond flora, Mystical Terrain integrates procedural landscape features that reshape world topography into dramatic vistas.

## 🏔️ Monolithic Spires & Karsts

In biomes such as **Spire Karst** and **Titan Peaks**, vertical terrain noise generators inject steep stone spires reaching from sea level up to building heights ($Y = 220+$). These formations are carved with water erosion channels and hollow inner chambers containing rare mineral geodes.

## 🪨 Hollow Boulders & Rock Shelters

Ancient glacial boulders dot the plains and highlands. These structures feature:
- Weathered stone outer shells (Ashstone, Sandrock, Rimestone, and Gloomstone).
- Internal mossy hollows serving as natural player campsites or dungeon entrances.
- Buried mineral caches beneath the central anchor block.

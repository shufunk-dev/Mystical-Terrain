# Overhauled Void Dragon Encounter

Mystical Terrain transforms the legacy Minecraft Ender Dragon fight into the **Void Dragon**—a terrifying, cinematic endgame encounter featuring complete GeckoLib geometry, aerial strafing, dynamic landing phases, and an authentic ritual summoning loop.

## 🐉 Encounter Highlights

- **Custom GeckoLib Mesh**: Replaces the ancient vanilla cuboid model with an animated, skeletal dragon armature featuring breathing animations, wing membrane flexing, and dynamic jaw movements.
- **Dynamic Aerial Strafing**: The Void Dragon actively tracks player clusters from high orbit, firing concentrated void breath clusters that linger on bedrock pillars.
- **Interactive Sitting Phase**: Upon landing on the central bedrock fountain, the dragon emits expanding energy pulses and deflects normal arrows with its wing carapace, requiring close-quarters combat.
- **Authentic Void Pedestal Ritual**: Rather than relying on vanilla End Crystals alone, the encounter can be summoned via the **Void Pedestal** network using Eyes of the Void.

## 🔮 Summoning Ritual

1. Locate the four ancient **Void Pedestals** situated symmetrically around the central Exit Portal in The End.
2. Place an **Eye of the Void** into each of the four pedestals:
   - Chat announces ritual progression: *"The void stirs as the eyes align..."*
   - Vertical purple beam beacons shoot into the sky, tethering to the central obsidian fountain.
3. The Void Dragon emerges with an explosion of void particles and roaring sound effects.

## ⚔️ Void Dragon Drops & Rewards

Defeating the Void Dragon unlocks tier-highest void armament and armor:

- **Void Dragon Shards & Scales**: Used to craft the impervious **Void Dragon Armor** set.
- **Voidreaver**: An ultra-tier blade with intrinsic void teleportation and damage scaling.
